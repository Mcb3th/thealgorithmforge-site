Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Overview

The Algorithm Forge respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit thealgorithmforge.com or contact The Algorithm Forge through the website, how that information may be used, and the services involved in processing website inquiries.

Information We Collect

When you voluntarily submit the website contact form, we may collect information including:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your email address
  • The service you are interested in
  • Information you include in your message

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the contact form.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Discuss potential services or projects
  • Provide requested information
  • Communicate regarding an existing or potential business relationship
  • Protect the website and contact form from spam or misuse

The Algorithm Forge does not sell personal information submitted through the website contact form.

Form Processing

The website contact form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form processing service.

Information submitted through the form may therefore be transmitted to and processed by Formspree in order to deliver the inquiry to The Algorithm Forge.

Email Communications

If you contact The Algorithm Forge, your email address and message may be retained as part of normal business correspondence.

Submitting the website contact form does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails or newsletters.

Cookies and Analytics

The Algorithm Forge website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral advertising tools.

If analytics, advertising tools, or additional tracking technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain those practices.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry, maintain business records, provide services, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted or securely disposed of.

Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This website and its services are intended for businesses and adults and are not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites or social media platforms. The Algorithm Forge is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as the website, services, or privacy practices change.

The date shown at the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently updated.
