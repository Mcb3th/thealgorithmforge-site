Algorithm Forge is a technology company built around a simple idea: start with the problem, understand what the business actually needs, and build the right solution from there.

Our Story
THE STORY

DIFFERENT ROADS.
ONE FORGE.

Algorithm Forge wasn't built around a single service. It grew from years of learning how to solve problems across very different environments.

Founder Brett Wollam's path began in the U.S. Army before moving into information technology, system administration, cybersecurity, technical operations, and AI quality work. Each chapter required the same underlying skill: understand the problem, learn what is needed, and figure out how to make things work.

Algorithm Forge brings that approach into one company. Today, the work spans IT support, web development, custom software, automation, and digital marketing, giving businesses a practical place to turn when technology needs to work better.

AF
WHY THE FORGE EXISTS

START WITH THE
PROBLEM.

Businesses don't experience technology in neat little categories.

A website problem can become a marketing problem. A repetitive process might need automation. A growing business may need better systems before it needs another piece of software.

Algorithm Forge was built to look across those boundaries. Instead of forcing every challenge into a predetermined service, the goal is to understand what is actually getting in the way and determine what makes sense from there.

BEHIND THE FORGE

BRETT
WOLLAM.

Founder, The Algorithm Forge

Builder, problem solver, and technologist.

My background crosses military service, IT, cybersecurity, technical operations, AI quality work, web development, and digital marketing. The technologies changed along the way, but the part I enjoyed most never did: figuring things out.

I built Algorithm Forge around that mindset. When a business brings me a problem, I don't expect them to arrive with the technical answer already figured out. That's my job. We start with what isn't working, what could work better, or what needs to be built, and go from there.
